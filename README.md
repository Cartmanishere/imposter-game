# 🎭 Impostor

A social deduction party game for 3–12 players. One (or more) of you doesn't belong — can you find them before they blend in?

## How to Play

1. **Setup** — Choose the number of players, impostors, and a word category.
2. **Pass & Peek** — Pass the device around. Each player secretly looks at their word. Most get the *real* word, but impostors get a **blank** — they have no idea what the word is.
3. **Discuss** — Go around in a circle. Each player gives a clue about the word **without saying it**. Be specific enough to prove you know it, but vague enough not to hand the impostor an easy win.
4. **Vote** — Everyone votes on who they think is the impostor.
5. **Reveal** — Find out who was bluffing!
